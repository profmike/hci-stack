## Paragraph-level positioning

For each work in the primary problem-space ranking, write one complete working paragraph that:

- credits the work's claimed-and-demonstrated contribution;
- separately names any demonstrated-unclaimed operated-capability collision;
- states the checked method/evidence boundary when material;
- names the literal operational difference from the planned project;
- identifies what the project learns from or inherits;
- says whether the relationship is replication, extension, contrast, instantiation, or a test of
  the prior proposition; and
- ends with the strongest safe positioning boundary, including what the project cannot claim.

## Re-ranking triggers

- target-problem identity, target population, activity, or empirical waist changes;
- planned mechanism, progression variable, access state, timing, scope, or user control changes;
- a partial source becomes full or a closer verified work appears;
- the proposed contribution layer changes; or
- an author decision selects a different gap or comparison emphasis.
