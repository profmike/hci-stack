# Related-work contribution-strength audit

Status: author positioning choice pending

## Ordered ladder

1. **Tier 1 — capability:** the project may enable a consequential user capability the comparison
   does not.
2. **Tier 2 — experience or outcome:** the capability exists, but a meaningful human outcome,
   experience, or performance measure may improve.
3. **Tier 3 — cost or access:** comparable capability and experience exist, but deployment may
   become smaller, lighter, cheaper, or more accessible.

Research method is not contribution tier. Record comparison proximity separately and use `N/A` for
theory, reviews, motivation sources, components, exemplars, and internal artifacts.

Canonical row-level accounting is `prior-work-evidence-accounting.csv`. For every atomic
proposition, independently record author claim, demonstrated artifact/study, operated capability,
evaluated result, capability collision, and contribution credit. Capability collision may include
a `DEMONSTRATED_UNCLAIMED` operation; contribution attribution may not. Ideas and proposals remain
in `idea-provenance-ledger.csv` with collision and credit `NONE`.

## Mandatory relationship classification

Classify every retained work before assigning a tier:

1. closest contribution comparator;
2. secondary same-domain evidence;
3. adjacent system or analogous intervention;
4. complementary tool or current practice;
5. design foundation or inspiration;
6. motivation, theory, or review; or
7. excluded or unresolved.

A shared modality, device, sensing technique, personalization label, multi-user topology, or output
channel is insufficient. “Similar approach” requires a similar causal interaction mechanism.
