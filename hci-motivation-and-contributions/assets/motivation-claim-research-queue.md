`unsupported`. It also routes proposed-system hypotheses to later project evidence without treating
adjacent literature as proof.

## Completion check

- [ ] Every active motivation/problem-definition hypothesis or unsupported claim has a precise
      route and disposition.
- [ ] Every externally resolvable claim has a documented full-source search and contradiction pass.
- [ ] Candidate discovery was completed before final directness screening; no strict final-evidence
      conjunction was used as the only intake query.
- [ ] Every high-impact external claim has a populated facet matrix, terminal results for the
      required cross-disciplinary routes, and reconciled unique candidates from at least two
      independent retrieval systems.
- [ ] Every external-AI or generated research report has provider/model/version, exact prompt,
      timestamp, durable raw artifact/hash, portable source identities, and terminal dispositions;
      opaque session citation handles are not treated as evidence.
- [ ] Every plausible identity embedded in notes, references, reports, or ambiguity records has its
      own source-resolution row or a specific terminal screen-out.
- [ ] Non-title/non-author positive controls pass within declared screen depths, and a complete
      promotion wave produced zero new decision-relevant candidates.
- [ ] Every product/practice claim has dated first-party documentation and a version/scope boundary.
- [ ] Every author-evidence need names the smallest existing artifact or future formative action.
- [ ] Every proposed-system hypothesis names a Phase 2/3 test rather than borrowed proof.
- [ ] Rejected and superseded claims remain visible.
- [ ] Claim ledger, motivation evidence map, evidence-strength register, and reports agree.

When every box is satisfied, set status to `MOTIVATION_CLAIM_AUDIT_COMPLETE`.
