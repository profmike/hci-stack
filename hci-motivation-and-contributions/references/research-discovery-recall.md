# Research-discovery recall contract

Use this contract for every high-impact motivation, problem-definition, consequence, access,
learning, adoption, or failure claim. It prevents a strict evidence standard from becoming a
low-recall search filter.

## Keep two gates separate

1. **Candidate discovery asks whether a work could change the vocabulary, graph, boundary,
   counterevidence, or evidentiary role.** Intake is deliberately recall-first. A missing population,
   timescale, device, comparator, or outcome does not exclude a candidate at this gate.
2. **Claim admissibility asks what the opened full source can support.** Apply directness, method,
   population, exposure, outcome, causal-rung, and evidence-strength requirements only after intake.

Retain each discovered candidate as `direct`, `near-direct`, `mechanism-or-transfer`,
`problem-recognition`, `official-practice`, `contradiction`, `vocabulary-or-graph-seed`, or
`screened-out-with-reason`. A source may be ineligible for the headline claim and still be a
required discovery seed.

## Build a claim-facet matrix before querying

For each high-impact claim, split and independently expand at least these facets:

| Facet | Required distinctions |
|---|---|
| Population | target group; novice; inexperienced; non-user/non-gamer; age, ability, and access groups |
| Activity/task | focal activity; component task; neighboring task; discipline-native task name |
| Interface/control | device, mapping, action, control law, degrees of freedom, coordination demand |
| Acquisition stage | first use, onboarding, early practice, learning curve, time to criterion, retention, transfer |
| Outcome/failure | time, error, collision, confusion, workload, frustration, non-completion, attrition, abandonment |
| Context | platform, display, application genre, laboratory/field setting, assisted/unassisted condition |
| Evidence role | phenomenon, mechanism, practice recognition, intervention efficacy, outcome, counterevidence |

Populate terms from controlled vocabulary, venue terminology, index terms, full-text introductions,
methods, limitations, reference lists, and vocabulary-expanding candidates. Do not require all
facets in one query. Run exact/direct queries, disjunctive queries, single-facet and mechanism-only
queries, and adjacent-outcome queries separately. Quote variants are optional probes, never the
entire search strategy.

## Required route portfolio

For every high-impact externally researchable claim, document terminal results for:

1. a target-problem route using the target population and activity;
2. a component/task route that omits the proposed intervention and its preferred terminology;
3. an acquisition/outcome route covering first use, repeated practice, retention or transfer when
   those timescales are claimed;
4. discipline-native databases and venues outside the project's primary mechanism silo;
5. official-practice or accessibility sources when they can establish recognized barriers or
   current design practice, kept separate from efficacy evidence;
6. a natural-language experience route when the phenomenon is likely described in community,
   support, onboarding, accessibility, or practitioner vocabulary before it is operationalized in
   scholarship. Use these accounts only for problem language, hypotheses, failure modes, and query
   expansion unless a separate method justifies stronger use; record an explicit not-applicable
   rationale when this route cannot add discovery value;
7. backward references, sibling titles/DOIs, author lineages, and at least two independent cited-by
   routes for promoted seeds; and
8. an **independent retrieval challenger** using a genuinely different index, search system, or
   research model.

The challenger is a candidate generator, not an authority. Preserve its provider, model/version
when applicable, timestamp, exact prompt/query, filters/sort/screen depth, raw-result artifact and
hash, every unique cited identity, and a terminal disposition. Reconcile unique candidates from at
least two independent retrieval systems before closure. If an external report exposes only opaque
session citation handles, obtain a normal bibliography or mark every unresolved handle
`UNTRACEABLE_CANDIDATE`; never use it to support a claim.

## Promote identities wherever they appear

Extract every plausible title, DOI, report number, or canonical URL mentioned in:

- source-resolution notes and ambiguity records;
- full-text references, related-work sections, footnotes, and supplements;
- supplied drafts, bibliographies, AI reports, and search-result snippets; and
- product documentation and practitioner guidance.

Every extracted identity must resolve to its own candidate/source-resolution row or a specific
terminal screen-out. A title or DOI embedded in another row is not accounted merely because the
containing row is terminal.

## Recall sentinels and closure

After the vocabulary lattice is drafted, choose known relevant works as positive controls. Each
must be retrievable without its exact title or author, within a declared screen depth, through two
independent providers when feasible. Store the claim, non-title/non-author query, provider, result
rank/page, date, and repair. A failed sentinel reopens the affected route.

A high-impact claim search closes only when:

- every required route has a replayable terminal record;
- every candidate has a terminal evidentiary-role disposition;
- vocabulary-expanding candidates and all embedded identities were promoted and swept;
- unique candidates from the independent challenger were reconciled;
- positive controls pass or the residual miss is visible and blocks the affected negative claim;
- a complete promotion wave produces zero new decision-relevant candidates; and
- the motivation queue, search log, source resolution, evidence map, and readiness state agree.

`Not found` always means `not found under these dated routes, queries, providers, and screen
depths`. Do not write `no evidence`, `no work`, or `the field has not studied` while a required route,
candidate, positive control, source acquisition, or final zero-yield wave remains open.

## Late-find repair

A material source found after closure is a process incident. Record the missed route and exact
failure, add a non-title/non-author sentinel, promote sibling identities and vocabulary, rerun the
affected claim and contradiction searches, reconcile the independent challenger, and repeat the
zero-yield wave. Fix the reusable route or checker when the failure could recur; adding the one
paper without repairing discovery does not close the incident.

## Common invalid shortcuts

- using the final direct-evidence predicate as the initial search conjunction;
- searching only the proposed device, mechanism, platform, or venue community;
- ignoring natural-language failure descriptions because community accounts are not admissible as
  outcome evidence;
- stopping at one ranked page without a declared refinement or pagination rule;
- treating accessibility or official guidance as learning-effect evidence;
- discarding adjacent sources before extracting their vocabulary and citation graph;
- accepting a model-generated synthesis without a portable source manifest; or
- declaring a phase complete because fluent reports exist while canonical research gates remain
  unchecked, templated, contradictory, or open.
