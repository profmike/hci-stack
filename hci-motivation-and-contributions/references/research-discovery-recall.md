# Research-discovery recall contract

Use this contract for every high-impact motivation, problem-definition, consequence, access,
learning, adoption, or failure claim. It prevents a strict evidence standard from becoming a
low-recall search filter.

## Keep two gates separate

1. **Candidate discovery asks whether a work could change the vocabulary, graph, boundary,
   counterevidence, or evidentiary role.** Intake is recall-first: a missing population, timescale,
   device, comparator, or outcome does not exclude a candidate here.
2. **Claim admissibility asks what the opened full source can support.** Apply directness, method,
   population, exposure, outcome, causal-rung, and evidence-strength requirements only after
   intake.

Retain each candidate as `direct`, `near-direct`, `mechanism-or-transfer`, `problem-recognition`,
`official-practice`, `contradiction`, `vocabulary-or-graph-seed`, or `screened-out-with-reason`. A
source ineligible for the headline claim can still be a required discovery seed.

## Build a claim-facet matrix before querying

Split each high-impact claim into population, activity or task, interface or control, acquisition
stage, outcome or failure mode, context, and evidence role. Expand each facet independently and
beyond the project's own words, drawing terms from index vocabulary, full-text methods and
limitations, reference lists, and vocabulary-expanding candidates. Never require all facets in one
query: run exact, disjunctive, single-facet, mechanism-only, and adjacent-outcome queries
separately.

## Required route portfolio

For every high-impact externally researchable claim, document terminal results for:

1. a target-problem route using the target population and activity;
2. a component/task route omitting the proposed intervention and its preferred terminology;
3. an acquisition/outcome route covering first use, practice, retention, or transfer when those
   timescales are claimed;
4. discipline-native databases and venues outside the project's primary mechanism silo;
5. official-practice or accessibility sources for recognized barriers or design practice, kept
   separate from efficacy evidence;
6. a **natural-language experience route** when community, support, or practitioner vocabulary
   likely describes the phenomenon before scholarship operationalizes it — used only for problem
   language, hypotheses, failure modes, and query expansion unless a separate method justifies
   more, with an explicit not-applicable rationale when it adds no discovery value;
7. backward references, sibling titles/DOIs, author lineages, and at least two independent cited-by
   routes for promoted seeds; and
8. an **independent retrieval challenger** using a genuinely different index, search system, or
   research model.

The challenger generates candidates; it is never an authority. Preserve its provider, model
version, timestamp, exact query, filters, screen depth, raw-result artifact and hash, every unique
cited identity, and a terminal disposition, and reconcile unique candidates from at least two
independent retrieval systems before closure. A report exposing only opaque session citation
handles needs a normal bibliography, or every unresolved handle marked `UNTRACEABLE_CANDIDATE`.

## Promote identities wherever they appear

Extract every plausible title, DOI, report number, or canonical URL from source-resolution notes,
references and footnotes, supplied drafts, model reports, search snippets, and product
documentation. Each needs its own candidate/source-resolution row or a specific terminal
screen-out; an identity embedded in a terminal row is not thereby accounted for.

## Recall sentinels and closure

Choose known relevant works as positive controls, each retrievable without its exact title or
author within a declared screen depth, and store the claim, non-title/non-author query, provider,
result rank/page, date, and repair. A failed sentinel reopens the affected route.

A high-impact claim search closes only when every required route has a replayable terminal record;
every candidate and embedded identity has a terminal evidentiary-role disposition; challenger
candidates are reconciled; positive controls pass or the residual miss is visible and blocks the
affected negative claim; a complete promotion wave produces zero new decision-relevant candidates;
and the queue, search log, source resolution, evidence map, and readiness state agree.

`Not found` always means `not found under these dated routes, queries, providers, and screen
depths`. Do not write `no evidence` or `the field has not studied` while a required route,
candidate, positive control, source acquisition, or final zero-yield wave remains open.

## Late-find repair

A material source found after closure is a process incident: record the missed route and exact
failure, add a non-title/non-author sentinel, promote sibling identities and vocabulary, rerun the
affected claim and contradiction searches, and repeat the zero-yield wave. Adding the paper without
repairing the reusable route or checker does not close the incident.
