# Phase 1 constructive reviewer panel

Use multiple lenses to improve the research direction, not to imitate a final-paper review or
manufacture consensus. The project may have no design, system, study, or results yet.

## Review bundle and independence

Freeze one read-only bundle — starting-state inventory and supplied materials, motivation evidence
map and claim-evidence ledger, source manifest and search/citation-chain logs with full copies,
related-work matrix and quadrant variations, problem/gap/approach/contribution/lexical-spine option
packets, author decision log, research framing outline, and draft Phase 2 handoff — recording its
paths or hashes in `frozen-artifacts.md`. Give every reviewer that bundle plus one lens, and share
no reviewer output until all reviews finish. Reviewers make no edits, commits, source uploads,
participant contact, implementation, or invented analyses. Missing later-phase work is not a finding
by itself; a reviewer may instead name an uncertainty and the smallest investigation resolving it.

## Seven lenses

**1. Domain-expert HCI researcher.** Fidelity of people, activity, pain, current practice,
consequence, and terminology; credit to the closest research, products, and practices; and whether a
claimed new workflow stage collides with existing human intervention through speech, gesture,
demonstration, or coordination.

**2. HCI researcher outside the domain.** Whether a broad CHI reader can follow the problem and gap
hypothesis; names jargon, hidden assumptions, and an empirical waist too narrow for the claimed
field-level value.

**3. Technical HCI systems researcher.** Whether the mechanism could plausibly create the intended
experience — dependencies, failure modes, latency or reliability thresholds, safety and privacy
risks, smallest Phase 2 probes — without demanding results from an unbuilt system.

**4. Designer.** Whether the experience follows from real needs rather than technology enthusiasm,
and which formative activities discriminate among alternative concepts; for differentiated channels,
what shared awareness is lost as well as what recipient relevance is gained.

**5. Quantitative-methods researcher.** Whether motivating quantitative claims respect their
sampling, constructs, uncertainty, and causal limits; names estimands, comparators, and measures
only as far as researchability requires.

**6. Qualitative-methods researcher.** What remains unknown about experience, practice, and
mechanism, and whether existing qualitative evidence supports the claimed kind of knowledge; does
not nitpick coding procedures for an undesigned study.

**7. Academic researcher and popular-science communicator.** Whether the outline carries the arc
`larger concern → specific pain → evidence → state of the art → gap → approach hypothesis →
prospective contribution → broader HCI value`, a non-specialist can restate it, exact constructs are
defined at first use, every approach, feature, control, and contribution is introduced through the
human situation and value it serves before the mechanism, an intended value is not presented as a
measured benefit, and a claim-local qualifier is not replaced by a nonresponsive disclaimer.

## Required reviewer output

Each reviewer restates the problem, gap, approach, and prospective contribution in one or two
sentences; names two to four strengths to preserve; reports three to seven highest-leverage
uncertainties, each with a stable ID, priority (`direction-blocking`, `important`, or
`later-phase`), confidence, exact artifact/location, smallest resolving action, phase responsible
(`1`, `2`, or `3`), and what result would change the framing; and closes with the best defensible
problem-gap-approach-contribution package, the strongest reason not to invest, one way the work
could become more significant without unsupported scope expansion, and any tension with another
lens. Avoid sentence-level edits unless terminology changes the claim.

## Synthesis

After all seven reviews are frozen, create `synthesis.md` recording the reviewed bundle and
independence statement; a finding matrix keyed by reviewer and ID; grouped causal themes that keep
minority concerns visible; a disposition of `revise-phase-1`, `plan-phase-2`, `plan-phase-3`,
`accept-risk`, or `reject` with rationale; the smallest action, owner, and return condition;
conflicts needing author choice; and a readiness recommendation.

Review lenses advise; they do not outvote evidence or replace the author. A domain expert cannot
waive a validity problem, a methods expert cannot redefine lived practice, a systems reviewer cannot
make the technology the contribution, and a communicator cannot simplify away an evidence boundary.
